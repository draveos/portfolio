OVERVIEW

a drone's lap around the earth

An interactive 3D flight experience built with CesiumJS. An autonomous drone orbits seven landmarks across the globe, transiting between them at near-space cruise altitude — high enough to watch the curvature roll by.

  • TOUR — scripted world tour with chase / top-down / free cameras
  • MISSION — click-to-plan waypoint flights with live distance & ETA, locked controls while airborne
  • ENV — time-of-day lighting, haze, and altitude-aware rain / snow shaders that fade out near space
  • 3D — OSM Buildings & Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles streaming

CesiumJS · vanilla JS · GLSL post-processing · zero build step

All trajectories are procedurally generated. Inspired by my work on autonomous-drone simulation — contains no proprietary code or data.
